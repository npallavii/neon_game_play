How to play
  • Stand about 2–3 m from the webcam so your whole body, knees included, is in view. Make sure the room is well lit.
  • Hit each neon block with a hand just as it reaches you. The glowing rings show where to aim.
  • REACH UP: both hands above your head. KNEE: lift that knee high.
  • DUCK: squat under the red beam. JUMP: hop over the purple bar. STEP: move your whole body sideways, away from the wall.
  • The coach on the left shows each move just before you need it. Keep hitting in a row to build your combo multiplier.
  • Keyboard mode: A / D punch · Q / E high · Z / C knee · W reach · S duck · Space jump · ← / → step.
